Samsung Life Q1 Net Income Jumps 83.1 Percent to 1.24 Trillion Won, Beating Market Estimates

Published: May 14, 2026

Summary: Samsung Life Insurance Co. reported first-quarter 2026 net income of 1.24 trillion won (US$832.1 million), up 83.1 percent from a year earlier, the South Korean insurer said on Thursday, May 14, 2026. Operating profit reached 1.35 trillion won against 754 billion won a year ago, and revenue rose 75 percent to 14.71 trillion won, exceeding the average analyst estimate of 738.5 billion won for net profit.

The quarter in figures

In a regulatory filing, Samsung Life Insurance Co. said it posted 1.35 trillion won in operating profit for the quarter, compared with 754 billion won a year earlier, meaning operating profit grew by roughly 596 billion won, or close to 80 percent, year on year. Revenue rose 75 percent to 14.71 trillion won. Net income of 1.24 trillion won corresponds to the reported 83.1 percent increase from the first quarter of 2025.

Against market expectations

  • Net income of 1.24 trillion won (US$832.1 million) versus an average analyst estimate of 738.5 billion won, a beat of about 500 billion won.
  • Operating profit of 1.35 trillion won against 754 billion won in the first quarter of 2025.
  • Revenue of 14.71 trillion won, up 75 percent year on year.
  • The consensus figure came from a survey by Yonhap Infomax, the financial data firm of Yonhap News Agency.

Reading the result

The distance between the reported net income and the consensus estimate is the headline of the quarter: at 1.24 trillion won against an average expectation of 738.5 billion won, the result landed far above what sell-side analysts had modelled. The operating line tells a similar story of scale, with 1.35 trillion won against 754 billion won a year earlier, while the 75 percent revenue expansion to 14.71 trillion won shows the top line moving in the same direction as profitability rather than against it. For a life insurer, a quarter in which revenue, operating profit and net income all expand by wide margins at once points to favourable conditions across both the insurance book and the investment side of the balance sheet, as reported in the filing.

Company information

Samsung Life Insurance Co. is a South Korean life insurance company headquartered in Seoul, South Korea, and one of the country's largest insurers by assets and premiums. Corporate website: https://www.samsunglife.com.
