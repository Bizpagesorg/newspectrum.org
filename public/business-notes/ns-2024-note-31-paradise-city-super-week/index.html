Paradise City Opens 2024 Paradise Super Week With Room Discounts of Up to 68 Percent

Published: May 16, 2024

Summary: Integrated resort complex Paradise City, in Jung-gu, Incheon, South Korea, is offering its annual blow-out discount and sales promotion period called Paradise Super Week until May 22, 2024, with room discounts of up to 68 percent and bundled access to the resort's indoor theme park, spa and dining facilities.

The 2024 promotion

2024 Paradise Super Week, which started on Monday, May 13, 2024, is the resort's iconic annual promotion allowing its guests to enhance their hotel experience with various benefits and rewards. This year's package includes a discount of up to 68 percent for Paradise City resort rooms.

Guests can schedule their stays at Paradise City Hotel, located in Jung-gu, Incheon, until July 14, 2024. The reservations for Art Paradiso, Paradise City's luxury boutique hotel line, and Paradise Hotel Busan can be made until Aug. 31, 2024, allowing guests to enjoy discounted room prices during the upcoming summer holiday.

What the packages include

  • Paradise City Hotel one-day and two-day packages: free access to the night-themed indoor theme park Wonderbox, the spa facility Cimer, a sauna and free breakfast at the resort's premium buffet On the Plate.
  • Art Paradiso: a Cimer promotion with Aqua Spa admission for two people plus a body wash and body scrub set; a Wonderbox package with free passes for two guests, character-themed hairbands and helium balloons.
  • Paradise Hotel Busan, in the city's celebrated Haeundae-gu: discount coupons, free draft beer, a free On the Plate breakfast, 50,000 won (about $36.74) in resort money usable at all hotel facilities, and limited-edition Hello Kitty hand cream, depending on the level of the package.

Why the promotion matters for the resort

Super Week concentrates the resort's three hotel brands — the flagship Paradise City Hotel, the boutique Art Paradiso line and Paradise Hotel Busan — into a single booking window ahead of the summer season, converting discounted room rates into bundled spend across Wonderbox, Cimer and the On the Plate restaurants. The resort-money element at the Busan property is designed to keep that spend inside the complex, while the long booking tails (July 14 for Incheon stays, Aug. 31 for Art Paradiso and Busan reservations) stretch the promotion's effect across the whole summer holiday period.

Company information

Paradise City is an integrated resort complex in Jung-gu, Incheon, South Korea, with hotels, the Wonderbox indoor theme park, the Cimer spa and dining venues, developed and operated under the Paradise brand. Corporate website: https://p-city.com.

Contact
