Become an Author

Who we look for

We welcome contributions from journalists, analysts and practitioners with first-hand knowledge of business, economics or finance.

What to send

A short pitch (2–3 sentences), your background, and one writing sample. Texts should be original and not published elsewhere.

How we work with authors

The editor reviews pitches within ten working days. Accepted materials go through fact-checking and editing before publication. Author names appear with their materials.

Where to write

Send pitches to info@newspectrum.org with “Author pitch” in the subject line.
